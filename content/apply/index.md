## Research and Collaboration Opportunities

I welcome motivated students, researchers, and technical collaborators interested in scalable geospatial data systems, GeoAI, and intelligent data management for smart cities and environmental applications.

### Areas of Interest

- Geospatial data science and GeoAI
- Spatial and spatiotemporal databases
- Cloud and Edge computing
- Approximate query processing and stream analytics
- Large geospatial models and intelligent data management
- Smart city and environmental mobility applications
